# 💧 FlowJob — Plumbing Business Manager

A complete business management PWA for plumbing professionals.

**Features:**
- 📊 Dashboard with business overview
- 📅 Job scheduling & calendar
- 💰 Quotes & invoicing
- 👥 Customer management
- 📷 Job photo capture (auto-compressed)
- 🛡️ Warranty tracking
- 🔧 Maintenance plans
- 📧 Email follow-ups
- 📱 Works offline (PWA)
- 🖨️ Print-ready documents
- 💳 Memberships & recurring billing
- ⭐ Review reputation management
- 📋 Contracts & legal documents
- 📸 Insurance claim documentation
- 👷 Employee management
- 📊 Analytics & reporting

**Live:** [flowjob-app.vercel.app](https://flowjob-app.vercel.app)

Built with vanilla JS — no build step, no dependencies.
